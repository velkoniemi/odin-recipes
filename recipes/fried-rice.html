Fried Rice

Description

Fried rice is a classic asian food that many people love. This fried rice recipe is very simple but delicious. It's also a good way to make use of your leftover food in the fridge.

Ingredients

  • 200 grams cooked and chilled white rice
  • 2 tablespoon neutral oil
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 4 scallions
  • 1/2 cup of fried shallot
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon white pepper
  • 1 teaspoon MSG
  • 100g any leftover meat (optional)
  • 50g diced carrot (optional)
  • 50g peas (optional)

Steps

  1. Chop the garlic and the scallions.
  2. Beat the egg well.
  3. Get a carbon steel wok and heat it up well.
  4. Pour the oil and swirl the wok to coat it.
  5. Add the garlic and the white part of the scallion.
  6. Add the carrot, peas, and leftover meat if you have it.
  7. After the aromatic becomes fragrant, put the egg into the wok and punch the egg with the ladle into tiny pieces.
  8. Throw the rice into the wok and punch it again with the ladle to avoid clumping.
  9. Pour the soy sauce onto the side of the wok, and add the white pepper and MSG.
  10. Keep frying until the rice is lightly charred, then sprinkle it with the green part of scallions.
  11. Plate the fried rice, and sprinkle it with fried shallots.
  12. Enjoy!
