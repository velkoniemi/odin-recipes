Oatmeal

Description

An easy to make high protein oatmeal. It is a perfect food for gym goers. Besides the high level protein, there are also healthy fats and fiber. All you need is a microwave to make this amazing meal.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup instant oats
  • 200ml of plain milk
  • 1 tablespoon chia seeds
  • 1 tablespoon peanut butter
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 1 cup protein powder
  • 1 cup berries (optional)
  • 1 cup nuts (optional)

Steps

  1. Add the oats into a bowl.
  2. Scatter the chia seeds on top of the oats evenly.
  3. Pour the milk until all of the oats and chia seeds are submerged.
  4. Microwave them for 1 minute at 800 watt.
  5. Using two different spoons, get a scoop of peanut butter and honey, and add them to the oatmeal.
  6. Add the protein powder.
  7. Stir well.
  8. If it's too heavy, add milk again until you get the desired consistency.
  9. If you have them, spread the berries and nuts on top of the oatmeal.
  10. Enjoy!
